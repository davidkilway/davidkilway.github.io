Panasonic ZS8 Sensor Cleaning

I wanted to clean my Panasonic point and shoot because it had visible dust spots that showed up in every photo. I disassembled it down to the sensor level, characterized the contamination using flat field imaging before touching anything, and cleared the loose debris with a bike pump. On reassembly the camera was entirely nonfunctional. I suspected a delicate ribbon cable was either damaged or not seated properly. Diagnosing that through systematic elimination taught me more about how compact camera internals work than the cleaning itself did.

Overview
Stripped down to the sensor, cleaned, and characterized with flat field imaging at f6.3 using a CFML36 CFL bulb.

Build Log
Aug 2026
Shot a flat field image at f6.3 against a CFML36 CFL bulb to figure out where the dust was sitting before opening anything. Spots stayed fixed across zoom levels which meant they were on the sensor plane and not inside the lens. Looked up the ZS8 before touching a single screw and found it uses JIS screws throughout. Important distinction since Phillips drivers would have stripped them.
Aug 2026
Opened it up using a PH00 screwdriver and a 1.5mm flat head to release the ribbon cable clips. Got down to the sensor level and disconnected two ribbon cables, the LCD and the main board connection. Photographed both connectors before touching them. Cleared the loose debris off the sensor with a bike pump. Put it back together.
Aug 2026
Powered on and the screen was completely dead and the lens was only partially initializing. Pulled the sensor out entirely and tested again and got the same behavior, which took the sensor out of the equation. Went back in and reseated every ribbon cable carefully. The LCD ribbon had not been fully locked down the first time around. Camera came back fully on the next power on. Shot another flat field and the spots were gone.
